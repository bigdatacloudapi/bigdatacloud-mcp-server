Hand off

Copy the briefing into a Claude conversation. Claude can cross-reference these names against urlscan.io and current reporting, attribute them to the apps that contact them, and tell you which are worth blocking — this page cannot reach the network to do that itself.

Nothing here is a clean bill of health. This page has no network access, so it can only judge the shape of a name — not its reputation. A name with no signal is one this analysis had nothing to say about, which is the normal state for first-party telemetry and means neither safe nor unsafe.

Paste or drop a Control D, Pi-hole, AdGuard or pfSense export — or any CSV with a column of hostnames. Columns are detected from what the cells contain, so nothing needs configuring first.
